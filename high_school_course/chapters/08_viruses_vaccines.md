# 8. Viruses, Vaccines, and Immune Recognition

## Nobel connection

David Baltimore, Renato Dulbecco, and Howard Temin (1975) clarified relationships between tumor viruses and genetic material. Peter Doherty and Rolf Zinkernagel (1996) explained how immune cells recognize virus-infected cells. Katalin Karikó and Drew Weissman (2023) contributed discoveries enabling effective mRNA vaccines.

## Foundation

Viruses depend on host cells for reproduction. Immune recognition is specific but constrained by the molecules a cell displays and by the history of immune exposure. A vaccine trains immune memory without requiring the disease itself.
