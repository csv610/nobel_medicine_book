# Explore: Georges J.F. K"ohler

**Scientist:** Georges J.F. K"ohler
**Research focus:** for theories concerning the specificity in development and control of the immune system and the discovery of the principle for production of monoclonal antibodies

**Subfield:** Infection, Immunity, and Vaccines

> **Why this matters:** A life-saving defense can begin with something invisible: scientists can infer a microbe or immune response from patterns of illness and protection.
>
> As your mentor, ask: What would count as evidence of protection, transmission, or causation—and what alternative explanation must be ruled out?

## Project

Construct a labeled paper model or digital diagram of the biological structure or pathway. Have a partner test whether the model predicts a new case, then revise it using evidence.

**Report:** state your question, show your method and raw observations, include a graph or diagram, explain uncertainty, and identify one ethical safeguard. Do not use human samples, animals, cultures, medicines, or patient information.
