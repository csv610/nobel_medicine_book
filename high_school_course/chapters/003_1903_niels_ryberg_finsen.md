# Niels Ryberg Finsen: Nobel Medicine, 1903

**Nobel Prize year:** 1903  
**Laureate:** Niels Ryberg Finsen  
**Nobel-recognized contribution:** for his contribution to the treatment of diseases, especially lupus vulgaris

Start with the [course foundations](../foundations.md) and [safety and ethics guide](../safety_ethics.md). Then read the relevant source chapter in the main book: [1903 prize chapter](../../chapters/1903_Finsen.tex). Look for the question, method, evidence, uncertainty, and later impact.

## Project

Create a safe spreadsheet transmission or treatment-evidence model. Define a comparison group, vary one assumption, graph the outcome, and explain what the model cannot prove.

**Report:** state your question, show your method and raw observations, include a graph or diagram, explain uncertainty, and identify one ethical safeguard. Do not use human samples, animals, cultures, medicines, or patient information.
