# Explore: Niels Ryberg Finsen

**Scientist:** Niels Ryberg Finsen
**Research focus:** for his contribution to the treatment of diseases, especially lupus vulgaris

**Subfield:** Therapeutics, Surgery, and Public Health

> **Why this matters:** Modern medicine often begins with a stubborn puzzle: why did one patient improve while others did not?
>
> As your mentor, ask: Who was compared with whom, what benefit was measured, what harms were tracked, and who was left out?

## Project

Create a safe spreadsheet transmission or treatment-evidence model. Define a comparison group, vary one assumption, graph the outcome, and explain what the model cannot prove.

**Report:** state your question, show your method and raw observations, include a graph or diagram, explain uncertainty, and identify one ethical safeguard. Do not use human samples, animals, cultures, medicines, or patient information.
