# 5. Food, Oxygen, and Cellular Energy

## Nobel connection

Archibald Hill and Otto Meyerhof (1922) studied heat production and muscle metabolism. Hans Krebs and Fritz Lipmann (1953) clarified central metabolic pathways and coenzyme A. Their work links movement, oxygen use, chemical reactions, and energy transfer.

## Foundation

Cells transfer energy through linked reactions. A pathway can be regulated, branched, and affected by oxygen availability. “More energy” is not a single measurement: heat, chemical energy, work, and rate are different quantities.

## Project: Human-safe energy measurement

Use a household object such as a toy car or marble ramp. Change one factor, such as ramp height, and measure travel distance or time. Repeat at least five times. Calculate a mean and range, then explain why mechanical output is only an analogy for cellular metabolism.

**Deliverable:** raw data, graph, uncertainty statement, and one proposed improvement.

## Discuss

Why can a muscle produce heat even when no visible movement occurs? What evidence would distinguish oxygen-limited from fuel-limited performance?
