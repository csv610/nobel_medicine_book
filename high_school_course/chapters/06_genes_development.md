# 6. Genes, Development, and Change

## Nobel connection

Thomas Hunt Morgan (1933) used fruit flies to connect genes with chromosomes. Barbara McClintock (1983) discovered mobile genetic elements. John Gurdon and Shinya Yamanaka (2012) showed that mature cells can be reprogrammed toward a stem-cell state.

## Foundation

Genes influence traits through networks, environments, and development; they do not act as simple destiny switches. A mutation can change a sequence, regulation, or protein function. Reprogramming a cell changes its state but does not erase all biological history or guarantee safe medical use.

## Project: Model inheritance and selection

Use coins or a random-number generator to model inheritance of two alleles. Run several families, calculate genotype and phenotype frequencies, and compare small and large populations. Add a selection rule and observe how results vary by chance.

**Deliverable:** frequency graphs and an explanation of why one simulation does not establish a universal law.

## Discuss

What is the difference between inheritance, gene expression, and genetic engineering? What safeguards should be required before a laboratory discovery becomes a treatment?
