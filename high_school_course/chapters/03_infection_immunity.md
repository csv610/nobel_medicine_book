# 3. Infection, Immunity, and Prevention

## Nobel connection

Emil von Behring (1901) developed serum therapy against diphtheria. Ilya Mechnikov and Paul Ehrlich (1908) were recognized for work on immunity. These discoveries helped establish that protection can involve both soluble factors and specialized cells.

## Foundation

The immune system distinguishes patterns, but it is not a perfect machine. Exposure, vaccination, prior illness, genetics, age, and chance can affect outcomes. Population protection also depends on contact patterns and access to prevention.

## Project: Build a transmission model

Use a spreadsheet or paper network of 30 people. Assign each person a number of contacts, then model one infection under different assumptions: no prevention, a fraction protected, and fewer contacts. Keep the rules explicit and run several trials with different starting cases.

**Deliverable:** line graph of cases over time and a discussion of why a model is not a forecast.

## Discuss

What is the difference between individual protection and population-level protection? Which assumptions most change the result?
