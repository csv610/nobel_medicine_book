# 7. The Brain, Nerves, and Behavior

## Nobel connection

Andrew Huxley, Alan Hodgkin, and John Eccles (1963) explained key electrical processes in nerve cells. David Hubel and Torsten Wiesel (1981) showed how visual information is organized in the brain. Eric Kandel (2000) studied cellular mechanisms of memory.

## Foundation

Neurons communicate through electrical changes and chemical synapses. Sensory systems transform physical signals into patterns of activity. Perception is an active interpretation, not a direct copy of the world.

## Project: Reaction-time and measurement limits

Use a ruler-drop test with a willing partner only after teacher approval and consent, or use a computer reaction-time simulation. Perform repeated trials, randomize the order of conditions, and report the median and range. Do not interpret the result as a medical or intelligence test.
