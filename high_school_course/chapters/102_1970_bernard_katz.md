# Explore: Bernard Katz

**Scientist:** Bernard Katz
**Research focus:** for their discoveries concerning the humoral transmittors in nerve terminals and the mechanism for their storage, release and inactivation

**Subfield:** Neuroscience, Senses, and Behavior

> **Why this matters:** Your brain does not receive a finished picture or sound; it builds an experience from streams of electrical and chemical signals.
>
> As your mentor, ask: What signal was measured, where was it measured, and how could expectation or attention change the result?

## Read and connect

Start with the [course foundations](../foundations.md) and [safety and ethics guide](../safety_ethics.md). Then read the [extended background reading](../../chapters/1970_Katz_Euler_Axelrod.tex). Look for the question, method, evidence, uncertainty, and later impact.

## Project

Design a safe signal-and-response investigation using a computer simulation, ruler, image, or sound source. Repeat measurements, graph variation, and identify at least two sources of bias.

**Report:** state your question, show your method and raw observations, include a graph or diagram, explain uncertainty, and identify one ethical safeguard. Do not use human samples, animals, cultures, medicines, or patient information.
