# Charles Louis Alphonse Laveran: Nobel Medicine, 1907

**Nobel Prize year:** 1907  
**Laureate:** Charles Louis Alphonse Laveran  
**Nobel-recognized contribution:** for his work on the role played by protozoa in causing diseases
