# Explore: Charles Louis Alphonse Laveran

**Scientist:** Charles Louis Alphonse Laveran
**Research focus:** for his work on the role played by protozoa in causing diseases

**Subfield:** Cell Biology and Molecular Systems

> **Why this matters:** A cell is not a featureless drop of fluid: it sorts cargo, builds molecules, repairs damage, and communicates through molecular machines.
>
> As your mentor, ask: What structure or molecule is doing the work, and how would you know that it—not a neighboring process—is responsible?

## Project

Create a safe spreadsheet transmission or treatment-evidence model. Define a comparison group, vary one assumption, graph the outcome, and explain what the model cannot prove.

**Report:** state your question, show your method and raw observations, include a graph or diagram, explain uncertainty, and identify one ethical safeguard. Do not use human samples, animals, cultures, medicines, or patient information.
