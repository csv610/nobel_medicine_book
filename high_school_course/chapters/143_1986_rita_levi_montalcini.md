# Explore: Rita Levi-Montalcini

**Scientist:** Rita Levi-Montalcini
**Research focus:** for their discoveries of growth factors

**Subfield:** Physiology, Metabolism, and Endocrinology

> **Why this matters:** Your body is running thousands of feedback systems right now, even while you sit still and read.
>
> As your mentor, ask: What variable is being regulated, what is the sensor, and what feedback could create delay or overshoot?

## Read and connect

Start with the [course foundations](../foundations.md) and [safety and ethics guide](../safety_ethics.md). Then read the [extended background reading](../../chapters/1986_Cohen_LeviMontalcini.tex). Look for the question, method, evidence, uncertainty, and later impact.

## Project

Construct a labeled paper model or digital diagram of the biological structure or pathway. Have a partner test whether the model predicts a new case, then revise it using evidence.

**Report:** state your question, show your method and raw observations, include a graph or diagram, explain uncertainty, and identify one ethical safeguard. Do not use human samples, animals, cultures, medicines, or patient information.
