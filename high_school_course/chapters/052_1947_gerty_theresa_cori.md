# Gerty Theresa Cori: Nobel Medicine, 1947

**Nobel Prize year:** 1947
**Laureate:** Gerty Theresa Cori
**Nobel-recognized contribution:** for their discovery of the course of the catalytic conversion of glycogen; and for his discovery of the part played by the hormone of the anterior pituitary lobe in the metabolism of sugar

**Subfield:** Physiology, Metabolism, and Endocrinology

> **Why this matters:** A heartbeat, a thought, and a sprint all depend on chemical reactions that turn materials into usable energy and controlled signals.
>
> As your mentor, ask: What variable is being regulated, what is the sensor, and what feedback could create delay or overshoot?

## Project

Model a biological feedback or energy system in a spreadsheet. Compare no feedback, moderate feedback, and delayed feedback; graph the results and connect the model to the Nobel contribution.

**Report:** state your question, show your method and raw observations, include a graph or diagram, explain uncertainty, and identify one ethical safeguard. Do not use human samples, animals, cultures, medicines, or patient information.
