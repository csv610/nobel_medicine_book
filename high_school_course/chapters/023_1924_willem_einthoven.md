# Willem Einthoven: Nobel Medicine, 1924

**Nobel Prize year:** 1924  
**Laureate:** Willem Einthoven  
**Nobel-recognized contribution:** for his discovery of the mechanism of the electrocardiogram

## Project

Create an evidence map for the discovery: question, observation, method, result, limitation, and later application. Use a small safe dataset or simulation to test one prediction.

**Report:** state your question, show your method and raw observations, include a graph or diagram, explain uncertainty, and identify one ethical safeguard. Do not use human samples, animals, cultures, medicines, or patient information.
