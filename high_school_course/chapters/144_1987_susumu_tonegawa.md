# Explore: Susumu Tonegawa

**Scientist:** Susumu Tonegawa
**Research focus:** for his discovery of the genetic principle for generation of antibody diversity

**Subfield:** Infection, Immunity, and Vaccines

> **Why this matters:** A life-saving defense can begin with something invisible: scientists can infer a microbe or immune response from patterns of illness and protection.
>
> As your mentor, ask: What would count as evidence of protection, transmission, or causation—and what alternative explanation must be ruled out?

## Read and connect

Start with the [course foundations](../foundations.md) and [safety and ethics guide](../safety_ethics.md). Then read the [extended background reading](../../chapters/1987_Tonegawa.tex). Look for the question, method, evidence, uncertainty, and later impact.

## Project

Build a paper or spreadsheet model of inheritance, gene regulation, or sequence change. Run repeated trials, show the data, and explain how a model differs from evidence from living cells.

**Report:** state your question, show your method and raw observations, include a graph or diagram, explain uncertainty, and identify one ethical safeguard. Do not use human samples, animals, cultures, medicines, or patient information.
