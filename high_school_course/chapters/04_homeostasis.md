# 4. Signals, Hormones, and Homeostasis

## Nobel connection

Ivan Pavlov (1904) studied digestive physiology and conditioned responses. Frederick Banting and John Macleod (1923) were recognized for the discovery of insulin. Their work shows how signals coordinate behavior and body chemistry.

## Foundation

A feedback system measures a condition, compares it with a useful range, and changes activity. Negative feedback tends to stabilize a variable; positive feedback amplifies a process. Real biological systems involve delays, multiple signals, and trade-offs.

## Project: Engineer a feedback controller

Use a spreadsheet to model room temperature. Set a target, sensor error, heater power, and a time delay. Compare a system with no feedback, weak feedback, and overly strong feedback. Graph temperature over time and identify overshoot or oscillation.

**Deliverable:** three graphs and an analogy connecting the model to glucose regulation.

## Discuss

What can go wrong when a signal is absent, ignored, or present at the wrong time? Why should a model be tested against real evidence?
