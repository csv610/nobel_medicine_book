# 2. Seeing Cells and Tissues

## Nobel connection

Camillo Golgi and Santiago Ramón y Cajal (1906) developed and used staining and microscopy to reveal the organization of the nervous system. Their competing interpretations helped establish that the brain is made of distinct cells connected in networks.

## Foundation

Magnification makes an image larger; resolution determines whether two nearby details can be distinguished. A microscope image is an interpretation produced by illumination, preparation, lenses, and the observer.

## Project: Measure a hidden pattern

Place a printed grid or a textured object under a phone camera. Take images from several distances and lighting conditions. Estimate the smallest spacing you can reliably distinguish. Record the setup, have a partner score the images without knowing the condition, and compare results.

**Deliverable:** image set, measurement table, and explanation of magnification versus resolution.

## Discuss

How could staining reveal structure while also changing what is observed? Why is an apparently clear image not necessarily a complete picture?
