# Explore: Werner Arber

**Scientist:** Werner Arber
**Research focus:** for their discovery of restriction enzymes and their application to problems of molecular genetics

**Subfield:** Genetics, Genomics, and Development

> **Why this matters:** Almost every cell in your body carries the same DNA, yet cells can become bone, nerve, or muscle by reading different instructions.
>
> As your mentor, ask: Is this claim about DNA sequence, gene activity, cell fate, or inheritance? Keep those ideas separate.

## Read and connect

Start with the [course foundations](../foundations.md) and [safety and ethics guide](../safety_ethics.md). Then read the [extended background reading](../../chapters/1978_Arber_Nathans_Smith.tex). Look for the question, method, evidence, uncertainty, and later impact.

## Project

Build a paper or spreadsheet model of inheritance, gene regulation, or sequence change. Run repeated trials, show the data, and explain how a model differs from evidence from living cells.

**Report:** state your question, show your method and raw observations, include a graph or diagram, explain uncertainty, and identify one ethical safeguard. Do not use human samples, animals, cultures, medicines, or patient information.
