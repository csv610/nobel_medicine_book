# Explore: Charles Richet

**Scientist:** Charles Richet
**Research focus:** for his discovery of anaphylaxis

**Subfield:** Infection, Immunity, and Vaccines

> **Why this matters:** Imagine trying to stop an epidemic before anyone could see the agent under a microscope.
>
> As your mentor, ask: What would count as evidence of protection, transmission, or causation—and what alternative explanation must be ruled out?

## Read and connect

Start with the [course foundations](../foundations.md) and [safety and ethics guide](../safety_ethics.md). Then read the [extended background reading](../../chapters/1913_Richet.tex). Look for the question, method, evidence, uncertainty, and later impact.

## Project

Design a safe signal-and-response investigation using a computer simulation, ruler, image, or sound source. Repeat measurements, graph variation, and identify at least two sources of bias.

**Report:** state your question, show your method and raw observations, include a graph or diagram, explain uncertainty, and identify one ethical safeguard. Do not use human samples, animals, cultures, medicines, or patient information.

## Reflection

1. What problem was the scientist trying to solve?
2. What evidence made the contribution persuasive?
3. What would you want to test or learn next?
