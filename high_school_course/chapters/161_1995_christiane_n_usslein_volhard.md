# Explore: Christiane N"usslein-Volhard

**Scientist:** Christiane N"usslein-Volhard
**Research focus:** for their discoveries concerning the genetic control of early embryonic development

**Subfield:** Genetics, Genomics, and Development

> **Why this matters:** A tiny change in genetic information can alter an entire organism—and sometimes reveal a rule shared by all life.
>
> As your mentor, ask: Is this claim about DNA sequence, gene activity, cell fate, or inheritance? Keep those ideas separate.

## Project

Build a paper or spreadsheet model of inheritance, gene regulation, or sequence change. Run repeated trials, show the data, and explain how a model differs from evidence from living cells.

**Report:** state your question, show your method and raw observations, include a graph or diagram, explain uncertainty, and identify one ethical safeguard. Do not use human samples, animals, cultures, medicines, or patient information.
