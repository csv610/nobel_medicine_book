# Emil von Behring: Nobel Medicine, 1901

**Nobel Prize year:** 1901  
**Laureate:** Emil von Behring  
**Nobel-recognized contribution:** for his work on serum therapy, especially against diphtheria

## Read and connect

Start with the [course foundations](../foundations.md) and [safety and ethics guide](../safety_ethics.md). Then read the relevant source chapter in the main book: [1901 prize chapter](../../chapters/1901_Behring.tex). Look for the question, method, evidence, uncertainty, and later impact.

## Project

Design a fictional, ethical treatment study on paper or with random-number simulation. Specify outcome, comparator, possible harms, sample size, and what result would change your conclusion.

**Report:** state your question, show your method and raw observations, include a graph or diagram, explain uncertainty, and identify one ethical safeguard. Do not use human samples, animals, cultures, medicines, or patient information.

## Reflection

1. What problem was the laureate trying to solve?
2. What evidence made the contribution persuasive?
3. What would you want to test or learn next?
