# Karl von Frisch: Nobel Medicine, 1973

**Nobel Prize year:** 1973  
**Laureate:** Karl von Frisch  
**Nobel-recognized contribution:** for their discoveries concerning organization and elicitation of individual and group behaviour

## Project

Design a safe signal-and-response investigation using a computer simulation, ruler, image, or sound source. Repeat measurements, graph variation, and identify at least two sources of bias.

**Report:** state your question, show your method and raw observations, include a graph or diagram, explain uncertainty, and identify one ethical safeguard. Do not use human samples, animals, cultures, medicines, or patient information.
