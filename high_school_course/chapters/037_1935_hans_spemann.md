# Hans Spemann: Nobel Medicine, 1935

**Nobel Prize year:** 1935  
**Laureate:** Hans Spemann  
**Nobel-recognized contribution:** for his discovery of the organizer effect in embryonic development

## Read and connect

Start with the [course foundations](../foundations.md) and [safety and ethics guide](../safety_ethics.md). Then read the relevant source chapter in the main book: [1935 prize chapter](../../chapters/1935_Spemann.tex). Look for the question, method, evidence, uncertainty, and later impact.

## Project

Build a paper or spreadsheet model of inheritance, gene regulation, or sequence change. Run repeated trials, show the data, and explain how a model differs from evidence from living cells.

**Report:** state your question, show your method and raw observations, include a graph or diagram, explain uncertainty, and identify one ethical safeguard. Do not use human samples, animals, cultures, medicines, or patient information.

## Reflection

1. What problem was the laureate trying to solve?
2. What evidence made the contribution persuasive?
3. What would you want to test or learn next?
