# Explore: Robert G. Edwards

**Scientist:** Robert G. Edwards
**Research focus:** for the development of in vitro fertilization

**Subfield:** Reproduction, Aging, and Human Origins

> **Why this matters:** The story of being human is written across generations—in cells, genomes, bodies, and the environments that shape them.
>
> As your mentor, ask: What can the evidence show about biology, and what conclusions would go beyond the evidence about people or identity?

## Read and connect

Start with the [course foundations](../foundations.md) and [safety and ethics guide](../safety_ethics.md). Then read the [extended background reading](../../chapters/2010_Edwards.tex). Look for the question, method, evidence, uncertainty, and later impact.

## Project

Design a fictional, ethical treatment study on paper or with random-number simulation. Specify outcome, comparator, possible harms, sample size, and what result would change your conclusion.

**Report:** state your question, show your method and raw observations, include a graph or diagram, explain uncertainty, and identify one ethical safeguard. Do not use human samples, animals, cultures, medicines, or patient information.

## Reflection

2. What evidence made the contribution persuasive?
3. What would you want to test or learn next?
