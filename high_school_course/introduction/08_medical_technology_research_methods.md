# Introduction 8: Medical Technology and Research Methods

## The big question

How do better instruments and better study designs make new medical knowledge possible?

## Why it matters

A tool does more than produce a picture or number. It changes which questions scientists can ask and which hidden patterns they can detect.

## Core ideas

- measurement, calibration, and resolution
- imaging and signal processing
- controls, reproducibility, and error
- models, simulations, and inference
- the difference between measuring a signal and explaining it

## A surprising fact

Every measurement is selective: an instrument reveals some features while ignoring or transforming others. A clear image can still support a wrong interpretation.

## Try this

Measure a printed pattern or household object using different distances, lighting, or phone-camera settings. Compare resolution, repeatability, and observer agreement.
