# Introduction 5: Cell Biology and Molecular Systems

## The big question

How do cells organize materials, make molecules, communicate, and repair themselves?

## Why it matters

A cell is not a featureless drop of fluid. It sorts cargo, builds molecules, reads information, removes waste, and coordinates thousands of reactions.

## Core ideas

- cell structures and compartments
- proteins as working molecules
- molecular recognition and signaling
- transport, recycling, and regulation
- models and evidence at scales too small to see directly

## A surprising fact

Many cellular processes work through temporary interactions—molecules bind, release, change shape, and bind again rather than acting like permanent parts of a machine.

## Try this

Construct a paper or digital model of a cellular pathway. Have a partner use it to predict a new case, then revise the model when it fails.
