# Introduction 3: Neuroscience, Senses, and Behavior

## The big question

How do cells turn signals into sensation, movement, memory, and behavior?

## Why it matters

Your brain does not receive a finished picture or sound. It builds an experience from electrical and chemical signals, using incomplete and changing information.

## Core ideas

- neurons and electrical signaling
- synapses and chemical communication
- sensory coding and perception
- learning, memory, attention, and behavior
- measurement limits and observer effects

## A surprising fact

Two people can receive similar sensory input and still report different experiences because perception depends on context, attention, and prior learning.

## Try this

Run a safe reaction-time or perception study using repeated trials and anonymous data. Graph variation and identify practice, expectation, and measurement effects.
