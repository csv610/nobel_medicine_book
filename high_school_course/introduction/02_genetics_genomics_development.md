# Introduction 2: Genetics, Genomics, and Development

## The big question

How does biological information shape cells and organisms across time?

## Why it matters

Nearly every cell carries the same DNA, yet cells can become nerve, muscle, or skin. The difference often lies in which instructions are read, when they are read, and how they interact with the environment.

## Core ideas

- DNA, genes, chromosomes, and genomes
- inheritance and variation
- gene regulation and cell identity
- mutation, development, and evolution
- evidence versus genetic determinism

## A surprising fact

A mature cell can retain the same genome while changing its identity when its regulatory state changes. Information is not the same as destiny.
