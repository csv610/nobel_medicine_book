# Introduction 4: Physiology, Metabolism, and Endocrinology

## The big question

How do organs and chemical pathways keep a body alive, responsive, and balanced?

## Why it matters

Your body is running thousands of feedback systems while you sit still and read. A heartbeat, a thought, and a sprint all depend on controlled chemical reactions.

## Core ideas

- homeostasis and feedback
- hormones and signaling
- oxygen, nutrients, and energy transfer
- muscles, blood, and circulation
- rates, delays, and biological trade-offs

## A surprising fact

Stability does not mean stillness. Healthy systems constantly change while keeping important variables within workable ranges.

## Try this

Model a feedback controller in a spreadsheet. Compare no feedback, moderate feedback, and delayed feedback; look for stability, overshoot, and oscillation.
