# Introduction 6: Therapeutics, Surgery, and Public Health

## The big question

How does an observation become a safe, useful intervention for real people?

## Why it matters

A dramatic treatment story is only the beginning. Careful comparisons reveal whether a therapy helps more people than it harms, for whom it works, and what remains uncertain.

## Core ideas

- diagnosis and mechanism
- treatment benefit and harm
- controls, comparison groups, and bias
- prevention and population health
- access, consent, and fair distribution

## A surprising fact

A treatment can be biologically effective and still be a poor public-health solution if it is inaccessible, unsafe in practice, or applied to the wrong population.

## Try this

Design a fictional treatment study using random-number simulation. Define the outcome, comparison, possible harms, sample size, and result that would change your conclusion.
