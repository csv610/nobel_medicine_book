# Introduction 7: Reproduction, Aging, and Human Origins

## The big question

How do bodies and populations change across generations and deep time?

## Why it matters

Questions about reproduction and human origins connect laboratory evidence to identity, family, history, culture, and ethics. Biological evidence must not be stretched into claims about human worth.

## Core ideas

- reproduction and developmental change
- inheritance across generations
- transplantation and cellular compatibility
- ancient DNA and population history
- ethics, identity, and respectful interpretation

## A surprising fact

The history of a population can sometimes be reconstructed from tiny molecular traces, but those traces never tell the whole story of a person or culture.
