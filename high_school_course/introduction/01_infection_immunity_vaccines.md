# Introduction 1: Infection, Immunity, and Vaccines

## The big question

How do diseases spread, and how can bodies and communities reduce their impact?

## Why it matters

An invisible agent can change the lives of millions. Scientists learn about it indirectly through patterns of illness, transmission, protection, and recovery.

## Core ideas

- pathogens and routes of transmission
- innate and adaptive immune responses
- antibodies, immune memory, and vaccination
- individual risk versus population protection
- evidence, uncertainty, and public-health decisions

## A surprising fact

Protection can sometimes be studied without seeing the original threat directly: patterns in who becomes ill, who remains well, and how exposure changes can reveal hidden mechanisms.

## Try this

Build a spreadsheet transmission model. Change contact patterns and the fraction of people protected. Run repeated trials and identify which assumptions control the result.
