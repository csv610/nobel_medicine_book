# Safety and Ethics

This course is educational, not medical advice. Students must not diagnose, treat, or test medicines on themselves or others.

## Non-negotiable rules

- Do not culture bacteria, mold, or other microorganisms.
- Do not use blood, saliva, body fluids, needles, medicines, unknown chemicals, or patient data.
- Do not run experiments on people or animals. Anonymous observation of public behavior is not automatically ethical; ask a teacher before collecting data about people.
- Wear eye protection when using liquids or tools, wash hands, and follow local school rules.
- Stop any project that creates a hazard and ask an adult supervisor.

## Scientific responsibility

Ask who benefits, who bears risk, whose data are missing, and whether the result can be reproduced. Use respectful language for disability, illness, ancestry, and mental health. Historical research can contain outdated or harmful assumptions; identify them rather than presenting them as current fact.

## Consent and privacy

Projects using classmates, interviews, photos, or online information require teacher approval, voluntary consent, and no identifying details in the final report. A student may decline without penalty.
